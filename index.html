Memory / freshman year

A machine made of drawings

One of my earliest animation projects was making a zoetrope. I drew a sequence on a long strip of paper, placed it inside the machine, and turned it by hand. Seeing my drawings move was a memorable introduction to the relationship between individual frames, timing, and motion.

Unlike my Frames Out of Order project, which asks visitors to arrange a walk cycle, this experience asks them to create their own frames and discover what happens when a sequence is set in motion.
